AI firms and the US government are competing for capital:

US Treasury issuance excluding T-bills has risen to $5.06 trillion over the last 12 months, the highest since the 2021 record.

Over the same period, corporate debt issuance has surged to a record $2.64 trillion.

This brings total Treasury and corporate debt issuance up to $7.70 trillion, an all-time high.

This is increasingly driven by AI-related firms, whose bond issuance has soared +541% YoY in 2025, to $109 billion, and another +78% in the first half of 2026, to a record $194 billion.

In other words, corporate America is funding one of the largest investment cycles in decades while the US government is running historically high deficits, forcing both public and private borrowers to compete for capital.

This competition is adding to other forces pushing long-term yields higher, including inflation, Fed policy, and economic growth expectations.

The US bond market is entering an era of intense capital competition.

US median household income adjusted for inflation rose +$2,250 YoY in 2025, or +2.6%, to a record $87,460.

This marks the 3rd consecutive annual increase, totaling +$6,000, or +7.4%.

At the same time, the top 10% saw real income rise +$4,400 YoY, or +1.7%, to a record +$261,300.

This comes as rising stock-market wealth and low unemployment more than offset persistent inflation throughout 2025.

On the other hand, the bottom 10% posted a -$160 YoY decline, or -0.8%, to $20,010.

Furthermore, females earned ~84% as much as their male counterparts last year, up from ~81% in 2024, marking the largest annual increase in the female-to-male earnings ratio in data going back to the early 1960s.

Inflation is being partially offset by higher wages.

根据历史推演，需要考虑重磅利好发布，比如，双节期间，降低印花税率，推动牛市氛围回归。米联储加息落地，但是依旧是扩表的。并没有收缩。当前对流动性的收紧仅仅是价格上，而不是实质上。流动性收紧通道并没有完全落地。目前央行逆周期调节依旧是明显，人民币汇率破6.7，无视加息推动美元指数走强。

很显然有很强的独立性。因此，在之前财政注资大背景下，后续货币政策并不一定会按收紧走。央行对于收紧宽松的定义有所改变。需要认真读《求是》，潘行长对金融信贷宽松紧缩的全新定义。这是一则公开的金融科普。会影响后续政策的解读。创业板3750-3300这一个月，投机猖獗，存量博弈，减量博弈，淋漓尽致体现了，A股投机是人性扭曲的封建史。

极端的贪婪引导极端的投机。无法无天到更大的无法无天。在只能上涨盈利的市场，趋利避害明显，争夺流动性的过程，投机与投资本就难以共存，因此，投机等于做空。投机的极致，就是做空的极致。物极必反，当前，正是投机极致。换句话，当前也可能成为投资的新起点。

投资是增量市场，容错率高，持续性强。是以盈利推动，也是以国情发展需求推动。投资是国运红利的分配史。在A股择时，本身就是一种选择。未来演绎，随着米联储加息落地，对于加息预期的恐慌也落地。相关配套应对也可以循序展开。这是一个最好的时代，也是一个最坏的时代。

而命运在于你相信未来是什么。投机vs投资，一念之间。
